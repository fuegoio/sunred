This guide walks you through your first few minutes in Sunred: signing in, subscribing to a feed, and reading an article. You don't need to install anything — Sunred runs in the browser.

## 1. Sign in

Open your Sunred instance and sign in with your AT Proto handle — the same account you use on Bluesky or any other AT Proto app. There is no separate Sunred password.

If your instance has a default PDS configured, you'll see a **Continue with snrd.social** button (or similar) that takes you straight through. Prefer to use an existing account? Click **Login with a different handle** and enter it — for example `alice.bsky.social`.

Sunred redirects you to your PDS to approve the login, then brings you back signed in. See [Identity & sync](/at-proto) for how it works behind the scenes.

## 2. Subscribe to a feed

Click the **+** next to **Feeds** in the sidebar and paste a website or feed URL — for example `https://blog.golang.org`. You can paste a regular website URL; Sunred discovers the feed automatically.

Before you commit, Sunred shows a preview: the feed's title, description, and its most recent articles. If it looks right, click **Subscribe to this feed**.

The feed's title and site link fill in after the first refresh. New articles will start appearing in your timeline as soon as Sunred fetches them.

## 3. Read an entry

Your timeline shows entries from every feed, newest first. Unread entries have a dot beside them.

Click an entry's title to open the original article in a new tab — Sunred marks it as read as you open it. Prefer to read in Sunred? Click anywhere else on the card to open the in-app reader.

A few things to try:

- Click the **star** icon on a card to bookmark an article. Starred articles live in **Starred** in the sidebar.
- Click the **share** icon to share an article with people who follow you. Their shared articles appear in your timeline alongside your own subscriptions.
- Use the **search** box at the top of the sidebar to search across every entry by title and content.

## 4. Organize with folders

As your subscriptions grow, group them into folders. Click the **folder** icon next to **Feeds** to create one — say "Technology" or "News" — then move feeds into it from a feed's page using the folder picker.

Folders only affect how feeds are grouped in the sidebar; they don't change the timeline.
