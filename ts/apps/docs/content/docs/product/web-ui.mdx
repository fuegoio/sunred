title: Web UI Guide
description: Tour the Sunred web app — sidebar, timeline, reader, search, and settings.

The web app has two main areas: a **sidebar** for navigation and a **main pane** for browsing and reading. On mobile, the sidebar collapses into a drawer you can open from the top.

## Sidebar

The sidebar is split into two parts. At the top are your views; below them, your feeds.

### Views

- **Unread** — entries you haven't read yet, across every feed. This is the default home view.
- **All** — every entry, read or unread.
- **Starred** — entries you've starred.

Click any view to filter the timeline. A search box sits at the very top — type a query and press Enter to search across every entry.

### Feeds

Below the views, your subscriptions are listed and grouped by folder. Two small buttons next to the **Feeds** heading let you create a folder or subscribe to a new feed.

Click the **Feeds** heading to see only entries from your own subscriptions — shared articles from people you follow are excluded. Click a feed to open its page, where you can refresh it, mark all its entries as read, rename it, move it to a folder, or unsubscribe.

### Follows

Below your feeds, the **Follows** section lists the people you follow. Click the **+** next to the heading to open the **Find people** dialog and search for users by handle or name — on federated setups this searches across instances too. Click the **Follows** heading to see only articles shared by the people you follow. See [Social](/social).

### Account menu

At the bottom of the sidebar, your avatar opens a menu with **Settings**, a light/dark theme toggle, and **Sign out**.

## Timeline

Each entry card shows the feed, the article title, the publication date, and a short preview. Unread entries have a dot on the left.

- **Open the article** — click the title to open the original in a new tab. Sunred marks the entry as read.
- **Read in Sunred** — click the card body to open the in-app reader.
- **Star** — click the star icon to bookmark an entry. It appears under **Starred**.
- **Share** — click the share icon to share the article with people who follow you. Shared articles appear in their timeline.
- **Mark unread again** — click the dot on a read entry to bring it back to unread.

On filtered views like Unread and Starred, entries animate out as you read or unstar them, keeping the list focused on what's left.

## Reader view

The in-app reader shows the entry's title, author, date, tags, and a summary. It does not render the full article body — most feeds only carry a summary, and opening the original is one click away.

Use the back button or press `Esc` to return to the timeline. Links inside the reader open in a new tab. Use the star icon in the header to star the entry.

## Search

Search runs a full-text query across every entry's title and content. Type a query in the search box and press Enter; results appear in the timeline like any other view.

## Feed page

Clicking a feed in the sidebar opens its page: a header with the feed's title, description, and a subscriber count, plus that feed's entry timeline.

The toolbar lets you:

- **Mark all as read** — clears the unread count for this feed.
- **Refresh** — fetch new entries immediately, without waiting for the scheduler.
- **Move to folder** — reassign the feed to a different folder.
- **Rename** — give the feed a custom title in your sidebar.
- **Unsubscribe** — drops your subscription to the feed. The feed itself stays for other subscribers.
- **Open website** / **Open feed** — links to the site and its raw feed URL.

## Settings

Settings live at `/settings` and have three sections:

- **Profile** — your display name, cached from your `app.bsky.actor.profile` record, plus a social handle and bio other Sunred users see in search results and the Follows list.
- **OPML** — import subscriptions from another reader, or export yours. See [OPML import & export](#opml-import-export) below.
- **Tokens** — create and revoke API tokens for the CLI and scripts. See [Authentication](/authentication).

### OPML import & export

Bring subscriptions over from another RSS reader: export an OPML file from your old reader, then import it under **Settings → OPML**. Sunred subscribes you to every feed in the file. Export works the other way — download an OPML file of all your subscriptions to move elsewhere or back up.

## Dark mode

Toggle light and dark themes from the account menu at the bottom of the sidebar. The choice is remembered on this device.
