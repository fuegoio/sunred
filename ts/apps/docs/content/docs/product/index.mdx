Sunred is an RSS reader. You subscribe to websites, Sunred fetches their feeds in the background, and you read new articles as they arrive — in a web app, a terminal UI, or from the command line.

Your identity lives on the [AT Protocol](https://atproto.com) — the same network Bluesky uses. You sign in with your handle, follow people, and share articles. Your social graph belongs to you, not to Sunred.

## How it works

1. **Subscribe to a feed.** Paste any website or feed URL. Sunred discovers the RSS, Atom, or JSON Feed automatically.
2. **Sunred fetches in the background.** An adaptive scheduler checks each feed as often as it publishes — a daily blog is checked daily, an hourly news feed hourly.
3. **Read new entries.** Articles appear in your timeline the moment they're published. Star the ones you want to keep, mark the rest as read.

## Three interfaces, one account

- **Web app** — a sidebar, timeline, and reader. Keyboard-friendly, mobile-ready, with search and folders.
- **Terminal UI** — a full-screen, keyboard-driven reader with Vim-style navigation.
- **CLI** — manage feeds, entries, and tokens from the shell.

Everything is backed by a single [REST API](/api-reference).

## Concepts

- **[Feeds](/feeds)** — your subscriptions. RSS, Atom, and JSON Feed, organized into folders.
- **[Entries](/entries)** — individual articles. Each has a read status and a starred flag.
- **[Social](/social)** — follow people and share articles. See what the people you follow are reading.
- **[Identity & sync](/at-proto)** — your AT Proto account. Sign in anywhere, take your social graph with you.
