---
title: Identity & sync
description: How Sunred identity works with AT Proto, and how your data stays in sync.
---

Sunred uses the [AT Protocol](https://atproto.com) for identity. Your Sunred
account is your AT Proto account — the same one you use on Bluesky or any
other AT Proto app. There is no separate Sunred password.

## Logging in

Sign in with your AT Proto handle (for example `alice.bsky.social`). Sunred
sends you to your PDS to approve the login, then brings you back signed in.
There's no separate Sunred password — you use the same account you already
have in the Bluesky app (or any other AT Proto client).

You can log into any Sunred instance with your handle — you are not tied to a
single server. If your friend runs a Sunred instance and you have a Bluesky
account, you can log into their instance directly. Some instances also offer
to create a new account directly from the login page.

## Where your data lives

Your social data lives on your PDS as public records, not locked inside
Sunred:

- Who you **follow**
- Articles you **share**
- Feeds you **subscribe to**
- Your Sunred **profile** (display name and bio, cached from your standard `app.bsky.actor.profile` record)

Sunred keeps a local **cache** of these records so the app stays fast, but
your PDS is the source of truth. When you follow, share, or subscribe, Sunred
writes the record to your PDS, and other Sunred users see it through the
relay.

## Syncing to a new instance

The first time you log into an Sunred instance, it pulls your existing
`io.sunred.*` records from your PDS into its local cache so your follows,
shares, and feed subscriptions are all there immediately. After
that it stays in sync via the relay. Your profile (display name and bio) is
cached from the standard `app.bsky.actor.profile` record on your PDS.

State that is not part of your AT Proto records — which entries you've read,
your folders, your per-instance settings — stays on the instance where you
created it. Switching instances means your social graph comes with you (it's
on your PDS), but your local reading state does not.

## Portability

Because your identity and social graph live on your PDS, you're not locked
into Sunred. Another AT Proto-compatible RSS reader can read the same
records, and you can move your PDS without losing your Sunred social data.
