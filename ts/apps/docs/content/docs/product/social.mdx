Sunred is social. Identity runs on the [AT Protocol](/at-proto), so you can follow other Sunred users and share articles with them — the same social graph you already have on Bluesky.

## Follow people

The quickest way is the **Find people** dialog: click the **+** next to **Follows** in the sidebar and search by handle or name. Results cover people on your instance and, when a relay is configured, people on other instances too. Click **Follow** next to anyone.

You can also follow from a profile. Open any user's profile at `/users/{handle}` and click **Follow** there.

Everyone you follow shows up under **Follows** in the sidebar. Click a name to open their profile, where you can follow or unfollow them.

Follows are part of your AT Proto social graph, so they travel with you when you sign into another instance. See [Sign in and sync with AT Proto](/at-proto) for how that works.

## Share an article

Click the **share** icon on any entry card. Everyone who follows you sees the article in their main timeline, with your name attached.

Sharing is independent of read status and starring — you can share something you've already read, or star something you've shared.

## Read what others share

Articles shared by people you follow appear in your **Unread** and **All** views alongside entries from your own subscriptions; there is no separate Social view. Each shared entry says who shared it ("shared by @handle"), and if it comes from a feed you don't subscribe to, a **Subscribe** button adds the source feed in one click.

When you follow someone, Sunred backfills their existing shares into your timeline, so you don't miss what they shared before you followed them.

## Edit your profile

Under **Settings → Profile**, set the display name, bio, and avatar that other Sunred users see. These are cached from your standard `app.bsky.actor.profile` record — the same profile Bluesky uses — and changes are mirrored back to your Bluesky account. Your handle isn't editable from Sunred: change it in a Bluesky client and Sunred picks it up.

## Use the Social API

Share articles, list who you follow, and search for users through the [Social API](/api-reference?tag=Social).
