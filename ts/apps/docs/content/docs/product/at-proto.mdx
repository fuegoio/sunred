Sunred runs identity on the [AT Protocol](https://atproto.com) — the same network Bluesky uses. Your Sunred account is your AT Proto account, the same one you use on Bluesky or any other AT Proto app. There is no separate Sunred password.

## Sign in

Sign in with your AT Proto handle, for example `alice.bsky.social`. Sunred sends you to your PDS to approve the login, then brings you back signed in.

You're not tied to one server: any Sunred instance accepts your handle, so if a friend runs an instance you can log into it directly. Some instances can also create a new account for you from the login page.

## Where your data lives

Your social data lives on your PDS as public records, not locked inside Sunred: who you follow, the articles you share and star, the feeds you subscribe to, and your Sunred profile (display name and bio, cached from your standard `app.bsky.actor.profile` record — the same profile Bluesky uses).

Sunred keeps a local cache of these records so the app stays fast, but your PDS is the source of truth. When you follow, share, star, or subscribe, Sunred writes the record to your PDS, and other Sunred users see it through the relay.

## Switch instances

The first time you sign into an instance, it pulls your existing `io.sunred.*` records from your PDS into its local cache, so your follows, shares, stars, and feed subscriptions are all there immediately. After that, the relay keeps them in sync.

Not everything travels. State that isn't part of your AT Proto records — which entries you've read, your folders, your per-instance settings — stays on the instance where you created it. Your social graph comes with you; your local reading state does not.

## Take your account elsewhere

Because your identity and social graph live on your PDS, you're not locked into Sunred. Another AT Proto-compatible RSS reader can read the same records, and you can move your PDS without losing your Sunred social data.
