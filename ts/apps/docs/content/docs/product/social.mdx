---
title: Social
description: Follow people, share articles, and see what others are reading.
---

Sunred is social. Because identity runs on the [AT Protocol](/at-proto), you can follow other Sunred users and share articles with them — the same social graph you already have on Bluesky.

## Follow people

There are two ways to follow someone:

- **From the sidebar** — open the **Find people** dialog (the **+** next to **Follows**) and search by handle or name. Results include people on your instance and, when a relay is configured, people on other instances too. Click **Follow** next to anyone.
- **From a profile** — open any user's profile at `/users/{handle}` and click **Follow**.

The people you follow appear under **Follows** in the sidebar. Click a name to open their profile, where you can follow or unfollow them.

The people you follow are part of your AT Proto social graph, so they travel with you when you sign into another instance. See [Identity & sync](/at-proto) for how that works.

## Share articles

Click the **share** icon on any entry card to share it. The people who follow you will see the article in their main timeline, with your name attached.

Sharing an article doesn't change its read status or starring — it's an independent action. You can share something you've already read, or star something you've shared.

## Shares in your timeline

Articles shared by people you follow appear in your **Unread** and **All** views alongside entries from your own subscriptions — there is no separate Social view. Each shared entry shows who shared it ("shared by @handle") and, if it comes from a feed you don't subscribe to, a **Subscribe** button so you can add the source feed in one click.

When you follow someone, Sunred backfills their existing shares into your timeline, so you don't miss what they've already shared.

## Your profile

Under **Settings → Profile**, set the display name, bio, and avatar other Sunred users see on your profile. These are cached from your standard `app.bsky.actor.profile` record (the same profile Bluesky uses), and any change is mirrored back to your Bluesky account. Your handle comes from your AT Proto account and is not editable from Sunred — change it from a Bluesky client and Sunred picks it up.

## Programmatic access

Share articles, list who you follow, and search for users through the [Social API](/api-reference?tag=Social).
