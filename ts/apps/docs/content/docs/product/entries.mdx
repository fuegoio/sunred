An **entry** is a single article fetched from a feed. Each one carries a read status, a starred flag, and sanitized HTML content. Your timeline shows entries newest-first, including articles shared by people you follow, which surface alongside entries from your own subscriptions.

## Keep track of what you've read

Every entry is in one of three states:

| Status    | What it means                                                |
| --------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| `unread`  | New: you haven't opened it yet. Shows a dot in the timeline. |
| `read`    | You've opened it. Hidden from the **Unread** view.          |
| `removed` | Hidden from the normal views without being marked read.    |

New entries start `unread`. Opening an article marks it `read` automatically. Click a read entry's dot to mark it unread again, useful when you want to come back to something.

## Read an entry

Click the entry's title to open the original on its source site in a new tab; Sunred marks it read. To stay inside Sunred, click the card body instead; the in-app reader shows the title, author, date, tags, and a summary, with the full article one click away in a new tab.

The reader doesn't render the full article body, because most feeds only carry a summary.

## Star an entry

Click the star icon on a card or in the reader header to bookmark an entry. Starred entries live under **Starred** in the sidebar whatever their read status. Click the star again to unstar.

## Share an entry

Click the share icon on a card to pass the article on to people who follow you. Articles shared by people you follow appear in your **Unread** and **All** views alongside your own subscriptions. See [Follow people and share articles](/social) for the full picture.

## Search your entries

The search box at the top of the sidebar runs a full-text query across every entry's title and content. Type a query and press Enter; results appear in the timeline like any other view. For more targeted queries, combine search with read-status and starred filters through the [Entries API](/api-reference?tag=Entries).

## Mark all as read

To clear a feed at once, open its page and click **Mark all as read**. The unread count drops to zero and those entries leave the **Unread** view.

## Use the Entries API

List, filter, update, and star entries through the [Entries API](/api-reference?tag=Entries): filter by feed, folder, status, starred, or a search query, and paginate with `limit` and `offset`.
