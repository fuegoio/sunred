Sunred is an RSS reader. You subscribe to websites, Sunred fetches their feeds in the background, and you read new articles as they arrive — in a web app, a terminal UI, or from the command line.

Your identity lives on the [AT Protocol](https://atproto.com), the same network Bluesky uses. Sign in with your handle, follow people, and share articles. Your social graph belongs to you, not to Sunred.

## How it works

Paste any website or feed URL and Sunred discovers the RSS, Atom, or JSON Feed automatically. An adaptive scheduler then checks each feed as often as it publishes — a daily blog is checked daily, an hourly news feed hourly. New articles appear in your timeline the moment they're published: star the ones you want to keep and mark the rest as read.

## Three interfaces, one account

- **Web app** — a sidebar, timeline, and reader. Keyboard-friendly, mobile-ready, with search and folders.
- **Terminal UI** — a full-screen, keyboard-driven reader with Vim-style navigation.
- **CLI** — manage feeds, entries, and tokens from the shell.

Everything is backed by a single [REST API](/api-reference).

## Concepts

- **[Feeds](/feeds)** — your subscriptions: RSS, Atom, and JSON Feed, organized into folders.
- **[Entries](/entries)** — individual articles. Each has a read status and a starred flag.
- **[Follow people and share articles](/social)** — the social side: see what the people you follow are reading.
- **[Sign in and sync with AT Proto](/at-proto)** — your AT Proto account. Sign in anywhere, take your social graph with you.
